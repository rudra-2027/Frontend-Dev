Top 3 Travel Destinations

Paris, France

From its famous landmarks, like the Eiffel Tower and Arc de Triomphe, to its renowned cuisine, fashion, and history, Paris is an amazing place to spend a week taking in the architecture, artwork, museums, food, and chic neighborhoods.

“A sunset you'll remember forever.” — Alex

Rome, Italy

When in Rome, do as the Romans do – enjoying full-bodied wines, rich pasta dishes, creamy gelato, and appreciating famous works of art and architecture as you wander the cobbled and worn sampietrini streets.

“A sunset you'll remember forever.” — Alex

New York City, New York

Hit all the sightseeing hotspots, like the Empire State Building, Statue of Liberty, Times Square, Central Park, Metopolitan Museum of Art, and Broadway. Venture further into NYC’s vibrant neighborhoods to find great restaurants, bars, and shops!

“A sunset you'll remember forever.” — Alex

Travel Tips

  • Check visa requirements.
  • Book key activities in advance.
  • Pack layers for changing weather.
