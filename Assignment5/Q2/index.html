Welcome to Lakshadweep

Settled off the Kerala coast on the Laccadive Sea, Lakshadweep translates to ‘a hundred thousand islands’. It’s India’s smallest union territory — a paradise of coral reefs and turquoise waters.

About Lakshadweep

Comprising 36 atolls and coral reefs, Lakshadweep is known for its rich marine life and pristine beaches. The islands remain a hidden gem for nature and adventure lovers, offering snorkeling, diving, and kayaking experiences like nowhere else in India.

Top Attractions

Explore Agatti Island’s coral lagoons, visit Bangaram for serene beaches, and experience Kalpeni’s crystal-clear waters. The islands are also home to traditional fishing villages and untouched biodiversity.

Nature & Sustainability

Lakshadweep promotes eco-tourism, preserving its fragile coral ecosystem. Visitors are encouraged to travel responsibly, helping maintain its natural balance for future generations.
